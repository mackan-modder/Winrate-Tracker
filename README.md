# Winrate Tracker

This is a mod that tracks winrate and estimates the time to beat levels based on your most recent runs on each percentage. The winrate gets presented with labels in the bottom left corner. In the settings you can choose which labels to display. These labels are meant to help you more clearly see your consistency at the level. 

Three main labels are implemented in the mod:
- A label with you winrate from 0
- A label with your winrate from the percent you are currently at
- A label that predicts how long it will take you to beat the level from 0 based on your winrate throughout the level and the length of the level. 

More labels may be added in the future!

There is support for linking levels together using the menu with a percentage sign on the level page.

The winrate gets updated with a Exponential Moving Average and will lag behind your "true" winrate as you improve on the level. The winrate gets more accurate over time but keep in mind that it will always lag behind your true winrate so long as you are improving.
